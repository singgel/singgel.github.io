Source image bytes are unchanged. CSS crops only select the relevant logo from a header or avatar; they do not recolor or redraw it. The logos reflect the retrieved public brand assets, rather than verified historical artwork for each employment period. Company names remain available to assistive technology through the chapter controls.

Default to official standard-color artwork, preserve the complete company mark and its proportions, and verify it against the official source in both themes. Use a neutral backing and preserve the original colors without CSS filters. Give replacement images new filenames so cached artwork cannot mask a correction.

For a company wordmark layout, take the complete lockup from the official website with its original spacing and relative sizes.
